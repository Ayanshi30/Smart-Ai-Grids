# Smart-Ai-Grids
A Smart AI Grid is an advanced electrical power system that integrates artificial intelligence with hardware components like sensors, smart meters, and automated switches, along with software for real-time data processing and control. It continuously monitors electricity generation, transmission, and consumption, enabling intelligent decision-making across the grid.

The system detects faults instantly, isolates affected sections automatically, and restores power with minimal downtime, significantly improving safety and reliability. Using machine learning, it predicts energy demand, prevents overloads, and reduces transmission and distribution losses. It also identifies energy theft and inefficiencies in the network.

Additionally, a Smart AI Grid supports seamless integration of renewable energy sources like solar and wind by balancing supply and demand dynamically. Overall, it creates a self-regulating, efficient, and sustainable power network with reduced operational costs and minimal human intervention.
